# Controls and recipes

- Shift-right-click a tamed horse, donkey or mule to open its equipment menu. Hover empty slots to see their purpose. Horse equipment uses one row; donkey/mule cargo uses the three rows below it.
- In that menu, shift-right-click the lantern slot to toggle it. Right-click the lantern with Kelp Oil on the cursor to refuel it; the glass bottle is returned.
- Shift-right-click a placed lantern to toggle it. Right-click it with Kelp Oil to refill. Flint and steel relights a torch and resets its fuel, including while it is still lit.
- Held lanterns toggle with shift-use in the air. Inventory light stacks can be serviced by right-clicking them with oil or flint and steel on the cursor, as appropriate. Flint and steel costs one durability use per torch in the refreshed stack; split the stack if the tool is too worn.
- Right-click a held saddlebag to open portable storage. Equip it on a donkey or mule to access the same contents there. Dye it in crafting; dye does not change capacity.
- Use a milk bucket on an empty cauldron, then use three glass bottles to collect the three Vials of Milk. Drink one to completely remove one randomly chosen harmful effect; other effects are unchanged.
- Use a Fishing Trap item on river water. Right-click a placed trap to collect every stored catch; break it to drop stored items.
- Right-click a Spawner Eye to seek its matching spawner. A failed search does not consume the eye.
- Enchant a mob head using the third, level-30 enchanting-table offer to apply Sigil. Combine it with a Trophy Mount; place the trophy facing you and right-click it to attune. Its direction stays fixed after placement. Right-click another trophy you own to switch immediately, with an empty hand or while holding an item. The active trophy appears in your status effects with its name and trophy icon; hover over that icon in your inventory to see its benefits and drawbacks. You can have one placed trophy of each type at a time. Breaking a trophy drops nothing but lets its owner place a replacement of that type.

Recipe-book unlocks occur when the appropriate ingredient is obtained. New items and potion forms are also available in the Fantasy Feudalism Items creative tab. Additional recipes and detailed stats are listed in the README.

- Hover torches and lanterns to see fuel stages 3/3, 2/3, 1/3 or Empty. Each stage is one-third of the configured burn time. Soul lights show Unlimited.
- Craft torches with one flint and steel to restore full fuel. Each occupied torch slot supplies one refreshed torch per craft and costs one durability use. Shift-craft for bulk refreshing; the tool is returned until it breaks.
